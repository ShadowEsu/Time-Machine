Time Machine Privacy Policy

DRAFT. Not in effect. Needs lawyer review.

Last updated: [date].

Short version: your code never leaves your computer. We collect as little as we can, we never sell it, and secret values are never stored anywhere, even on your own machine.

1. What stays on your computer

The tm tool and the Mac app keep everything locally: save points, Git bundles, file copies, check logs, the audit log, and the names of settings you choose. We cannot see any of it. For settings, Time Machine stores only key names and a keyed fingerprint, never the values.

2. What we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Email addressYou join the waitlist or create an accountTo reach you about your account, and product news if you opt in

Telemetry is off by default. If you turn it on, it sends event names, durations, verdict codes, app version and OS version. It never sends code, file names, paths, commit messages, branch names, setting names or values, or anything from your check output. You can turn it off at any time.

When you join the waitlist, we store your email address, that you agreed to join, the page you signed up from, the time, your own referral code, and the referral code that brought you, if any. Your signup is saved as pending and unconfirmed. The site does not send you any email. Pending signups do not count toward anyone's place in line. How confirmation and any future waitlist email will work is not decided yet, and we will update this page before it changes. We also count page visits, link shares and link copies. Each count stores only the kind of event, the page, whether the visit came through a referral link, and the time. Counts hold no email and no referral code. We do not store your IP address or user agent, and none of this uses cookies. Everything is stored in Supabase, written by a function hosted on Vercel. To have your entry deleted, email preston@unvibe.site.

To limit requests, we store a one-way code derived from your IP address, not the IP itself. Codes older than one hour are removed in batches during later requests.

3. What we never do

  • Upload your code or project files.
  • Store secret values.
  • Sell or share your personal information for advertising.
  • Use your data to train AI models.

4. Who we share with

Only service providers that help us run Time Machine, under contract: payment (Stripe), email delivery, and website hosting. We may share information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Account data: while your account is open, then deleted within 90 days. Billing records: as long as tax law requires. Telemetry: 13 months at most. Waitlist emails: until you unsubscribe or we launch and you do not sign up within 6 months.

Anonymous website event counts older than 90 days are removed in batches when new counts arrive. If the site is inactive, older counts remain until it receives new visits.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data. Email [Contact]. We answer within 30 days. If you live in California, the EU, the UK or another place with privacy laws, you have the rights those laws give you, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

7. Children

Time Machine is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, least privilege access, and keep the data we hold small on purpose. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Where data is processed

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries, with legal safeguards for international transfers.

10. Changes

We will email you and post a notice at least 30 days before any material change.
