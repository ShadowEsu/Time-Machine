Time Machine Privacy Policy

DRAFT. Not in effect. Needs lawyer review.

Last updated: 28 October 2026. On 30 October 2026 we added the in-app survey and the waitlist's feedback email box. The parts you agree to at sign-in and checkout did not change.

Short version: your code never leaves your computer. We collect as little as we can, we never sell it, and secret values from config files like .env are never stored anywhere, even on your own machine. Only their key names and a fingerprint are kept.

1. What stays on your computer

The tm tool and the web app keep everything locally: save points, Git bundles, file copies, check logs, the audit log, and the names of settings you choose. We cannot see any of it. For settings, Time Machine stores only key names and a keyed fingerprint, never the values.

Two optional web app features talk to other services, and only after you turn them on. Ask sends a short summary of your history, with your own key, to Google's Gemini API, or to Anthropic's Claude API when only an Anthropic key is set. With a free Gemini key, Google may use what Ask sends to improve its products, and people at Google may read it (Gemini API terms). Anthropic's commercial terms cover what is sent with an Anthropic key. Vercel links read your deployments with your own token.

The app shows your survey ID after you press Send, and in Settings.

3. What we never do

  • Upload your code or project files.
  • Store the values in config files like .env.
  • Sell or share your personal information for advertising.
  • Use your data to train AI models.

4. Who we share with

Only service providers that help us run Time Machine: Vercel, which hosts this site and its functions, Supabase, which holds the waitlist database, survey answers and sign-in accounts, and Stripe, which processes payment once checkout opens. If we use an AI tool to summarise survey answers, it is Claude through Anthropic's commercial API, whose commercial terms do not let Anthropic train its models on them. We will add any other provider here before we use it. We may share information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Purchase records: for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Waitlist emails: until you ask us to delete yours, and we delete the whole list 6 months after launch.

Anonymous website event counts older than 90 days are removed in batches when new counts arrive. If the site is inactive, older counts remain until it receives new visits.

Survey answers: 12 months. A daily job deletes answers older than that. Each computer can send answers at most once in 90 days.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data. Email preston@unvibe.site. We answer within 30 days. If you live in California, the EU, the UK or another place with privacy laws, you have the rights those laws give you, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

7. Children

Time Machine is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, least privilege access, and keep the data we hold small on purpose. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Where data is processed

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries, with legal safeguards for international transfers.

10. Changes

We will post changes here at least 30 days before they take effect.
