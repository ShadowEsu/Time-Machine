Questions

Does it replace Git?

No. It works next to Git. A recovery is a new branch you inspect before you merge it yourself.

Why not the agent's own rewind?

Claude Code's docs say checkpointing does not track files changed by Bash commands. Source

Does a PASS mean my app is correct?

It means your tests passed. Time Machine never calls a save point working unless your own check passed on it.

What happens after I join?

Your email is saved. This site sends no email. Preston writes to the list himself when the CLI is on npm.

What will it cost?

Nothing is for sale. The CLI is free and MIT licensed. Restores will never be behind a paywall.
